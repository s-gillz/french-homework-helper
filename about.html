About French Learning Hub

Empowering French Immersion families across Ontario with innovative learning tools.

Our Story

French Learning Hub was created by The Study Zone to address a critical challenge faced by thousands of families across Brampton, Mississauga, Caledon, and the Greater Toronto Area: supporting children in French Immersion when parents don't speak French themselves.

We saw parents struggling to help with homework, students losing confidence in their pronunciation, and families paying high rates for private tutoring that wasn't always available when needed most.

Our solution? Combine artificial intelligence with proven pedagogical methods to create an affordable, always-available, curriculum-aligned French tutor.

Our Mission

"To make high-quality French Immersion support accessible to every Ontario family, regardless of their budget, schedule, or own French proficiency."
